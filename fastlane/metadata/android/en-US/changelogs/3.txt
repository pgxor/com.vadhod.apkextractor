First public release.

* Browse installed apps in System and User tabs; search and sort.
* Extract one app or batch-extract a selection, with live progress.
* Split-APK aware: single .apk, or a reinstallable .apks bundle.
* Inspect SDK levels, install dates, signing SHA-256 and APK contents.
* Share an APK or export an app icon as PNG.
* Saves only to the folder you pick (SAF).
* Pastel design with light and dark themes.
* No internet permission, no trackers, no analytics.
